# Independent capstone — Move the reading station without breaking the route

*Hand off a change somebody else can retrace.*

Work in a disposable copy or on paper. Begin without an assistant. Use only the original primitives or clearly permitted assets. The task is about a person finding a destination, interacting with it, and returning, not about autonomous character behavior.

## New brief

A new exhibit must be placed away from the northwest kiosk pad. In your copy, put the new frame at world `(-1, 0, -2)`, rotate it +90 degrees about world up, and attach a sign at local `(0.6, 1.3, -0.2)`. Place a viewing stand point from which the new sign is legible, and preserve a navigable route to the kiosk. Add one new obstacle or nonblocking decoration and label which it is. The north entry and ordinary east return must still satisfy their different movement contracts. Require a label and a cue not relying on hue for any new decision you introduce. Do not include route answers in the project source where they replace the learner's evidence sheet.

## Evidence sheet

| Question | What you must report |
| --- | --- |
| Spatial contract | Predicted world point from annotated axes, measured point and tolerance; one change that would falsify alignment |
| Readability | Spawn and new stand point at same zoom; cue as seen before choice; small-size or grayscale check; one access limitation |
| Movement and interaction | Approached center from both sides, reverse case, east return, and ordinary obstruction choice; overlap and stage trace after leaving and returning |
| Render cost | Baseline and changed setup held constant; exact adapter, resolution, renderer and units; repeat run with median and tail statistic or state why unavailable |
| Packaging and recovery | Clean source inventory, cold start instruction, known unverified behavior and one reversible step |

If you cannot run a 3D engine, provide a clearly labeled paper model, coordinate calculation, route/cue sketches and proposed test trace. A paper model does not verify physical contacts or frame intervals. An engine result does not replace a judgment about what a visitor saw. Use the two as separate kinds of evidence.

A peer reviewer checks whether the report makes sense without your explanation. Score 0–2 in each evidence row: unsupported, plausible but not checked, or reproducibly checked. A missing actual test can still earn credit as an accurately bounded paper result when the assignment permits it, but cannot be labeled a run. A scene that visibly works only because you moved the camera between trials must revise the comparison.

## Transfer

The visitor radius is now larger, and the new kiosk trigger should remain nonblocking. Predict the first evidence likely to fail. Name the entry/clearance or visibility condition affected, then rerun or rewrite one exact sequence under the new assumption. If using AI afterward, ask it to propose one failure case in your already completed design, not a solution to submit. Confirm it yourself and log what changed. Preserve the original working scene and give the next person a route that is still meaningful without you in the room.
